# Privacy Policy for Flybo II

*Last updated: November 23, 2026*

Flybo II: Drone Sky Destroyer (the "App") is provided by Albert Yulaev ("we," "us," or "our"). 

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the App, including information handled by third-party advertising services.

## 1. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account, provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or other direct identifying information to play Flybo II.

The App does not include our own account system or a separate analytics service. Game progress and preferences may be stored locally on your device.

**Advertising and third-party SDKs**

The free version of the App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display rewarded advertisements. The App also uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to request and manage privacy and consent choices where required.

When the advertising SDK operates, Google Mobile Ads may collect or receive information from the App or your device. Depending on the SDK configuration, device settings, your location, and your consent choices, this information may include:

*   **Device identifiers:** such as an advertising identifier or other device/app identifiers.
*   **Approximate location:** such as general location inferred from an IP address.
*   **Advertising data:** such as information about ads delivered or viewed and related ad interactions.
*   **Product interaction data:** such as app launches, ad views, taps, and interactions with advertisements.
*   **Diagnostics and performance data:** such as crash information, app launch time, stability, hang rate, or energy-use information.
*   **Network and technical information:** such as IP address, device type, operating system, and information needed to deliver and measure advertisements.

Google may use this information for purposes described in its documentation, which can include delivering and measuring advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and improving its services. The exact processing depends on Google's services, the applicable consent signals, and the configuration in effect.

[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) | [Google Mobile Ads SDK](https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/data-disclosure) | [Google UMP SDK](https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy)

We do not operate the advertising network and do not control Google's independent processing. Please review Google's policies for more information about its practices.

## 2. Privacy Choices and Consent

The App integrates Google's UMP SDK to request updated consent information and present privacy messages when required.

The choices available to you may depend on your region and the privacy requirements that apply. Where a consent message is presented, review the choices shown before making your selection.

Your device and operating-system settings may also provide controls related to advertising identifiers and privacy. The availability and effect of those controls depend on your device and the applicable platform rules.

For information about Google's consent tools, see [Google's UMP documentation](https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy).

## 3. Purchases

The App may offer an in-app purchase to remove advertisements. Purchases and payment processing are handled by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit.

We do not receive or store your full payment card number or banking details. The App receives purchase entitlement information needed to determine whether the advertisement-removal feature is active and to restore eligible purchases.

Apple's privacy information is available at [Apple Privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## 4. How We Use Information

The App itself uses locally available information to operate the game, maintain game state, and recognize whether the advertisement-removal entitlement is active.

Information processed by Google Mobile Ads and UMP may be used for advertising delivery and measurement, consent management, analytics, security, fraud prevention, and SDK performance, as described by Google.

## 5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell information that we collect directly from you.

Information may be processed by Google and its advertising service providers as part of providing advertising and consent-management services. Apple processes App Store purchases under its own terms and privacy practices.

We do not include Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or another separate third-party analytics product in the project configuration reviewed for this version of the App. If third-party services are added in a future version, this Policy will be updated as appropriate.

## 6. Data Retention and Security

Game data stored locally remains on your device and may be removed when you delete the App or clear its data, subject to your device's backup settings.

Google and Apple determine retention periods for information they process under their respective services and policies. We do not control their retention practices.

We take reasonable steps within our control to keep the App and its integrations appropriately configured. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is not designed to request personal information directly from children. The App's advertising services may have their own age-related settings and requirements. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us directly, please contact us using the details below.

Parents and guardians should review the applicable advertising and platform privacy settings for the device and region in which the App is used.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its SDKs, advertising features, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at the same URL, with a revised "Last updated" date.
